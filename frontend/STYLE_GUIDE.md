## Design system (3 Oct 2026)

- **Pages outside a project (SPEC-projects-03 FR-24, 6 Oct 2026).** Home, New project and the admin console use the same look as the app: the `surface-base` frame, Inter headings in the page-title style, and app cards. There is no paper. The only serif is the Home greeting (`font-serif`, Instrument Serif) and the footer wordmark drawn in it. Their small labels are JetBrains Mono, uppercase, 11px, `tracking-[0.06em]`. Project art (the dithered landscapes, the hero ring and the footer heron) is drawn on canvas in `features/home/art`. Its lightest tone is the frame colour, so it sits on the page without an edge. It respects reduced motion, and stops drawing while off screen.
- **Type.** Inter everywhere, loaded with its optical-size axis, so large text gets the Display cut on its own. Features `cv11`, `ss01`, `ss03` (single-storey a, open digits). JetBrains Mono for ids, code and logs. `font-display` is the same family, kept as a hook for headings and numbers.
- **Colour is tokens.** `src/index.css` defines channel triples (`--c-ink-1` …) for light and `[data-theme="dark"]`; `tailwind.config.js` maps `ink`, `surface`, `raised`, `elevated`, `line`, `brand` to them, so opacity (`bg-ink-1/5`) and hover variants work in both themes. Status families (emerald, amber, rose, violet, blue, …) come from `src/styles/palette.css`, generated by `scripts/gen-palette.mjs` from Tailwind's palette: light is Tailwind's value, dark keeps the hue with tints as deep washes and text shades light.
- **Neutrals** are a true grey (no blue cast). **Brand** is one iris accent (`#5B5BD6` light, `#7876F0` dark). Colour still appears only on status, priority, numbers and the one accent.
- **Surfaces, back to front:** `surface-base` (the app frame and sidebar), `surface` (the working panel), `raised` (cards), `panel`/`subtle` (fills inside cards), `elevated` (something that sits above its track, like the active segment). Use `bg-raised`, never `bg-white`.
- **Depth.** Light: hairline borders and a two-layer, very soft card shadow. Dark: no shadows; a lighter surface is closer. Shadows are tokens (`shadow-card`, `shadow-pop`, `shadow-modal`).
- **Shell.** The sidebar sits on the frame; the working area is one panel with a hairline edge and 12px radius, and is its own scroll container (`[data-scroll-root]`). Full-height pages use `h-[calc(100dvh-var(--shell-chrome))]`.
- **Labels** are sentence case. Uppercase is for table headers only.
- **Appearance** is Light, Dark or System (follows the OS).
- **Page titles (3 Oct 2026, round 3).** Every list page opens with a large title (30px, `-0.025em`) and one quiet piece of data beside it, rendered by `PageHeader` into the title slot at the top of the content. The 56px top bar keeps the breadcrumb and controls; it is clear over the top of the page, frosts once the page scrolls, and shows a compact copy of the title once the large one is under it. Full-bleed pages (a task, a skill, a knowledge base) and pages with their own hero (`compact`) keep the title in the bar.
- **Search and keys.** `Ctrl K` / `⌘K` opens the command palette (`CommandPalette`): pages, actions, tickets, tasks, boards, pipelines, skills, routines and knowledge, then "Ask Sonic" and "Start a task" with the typed words. Plain keys: `C` new task, `G` then a letter for a page (`lib/shortcuts.ts` holds the map the palette shows), `/` the list search, `Ctrl J` the Sonic panel. Plain keys never fire inside a field or over an open dialog or menu.
- **People** show as `PersonAvatar` / `PersonName`: initials in a soft tint picked from the name, so one person keeps one colour everywhere. The tint is identity, never status.
- **Stat tiles** are 32px figures in 16px-radius cards with 16px gaps; section headings on Overview are 18px with 40px between sections.

### Weights And Spacing

- Regular body text uses `400`.
- Most operational labels and rows use `500` or `600`.
- Cards and panel titles use `font-[720]` when Sora is used, matching the source app's heavier display treatment.
- Major page titles and hero numerals use `font-[760]`.
- Use `tracking-normal` for normal text. Display text only (page titles, figures of 24px and up) tightens to `-0.02em`–`-0.025em`, the way Inter Display is set.
- Uppercase labels may use positive tracking only: `tracking-[0.06em]`, `tracking-[0.12em]`, `tracking-[0.14em]`, or `tracking-[0.16em]`.

### Component Typography

- Buttons follow the source shared primitive: `sm` is `h-8 px-3 text-[12.5px]`, default is `h-10 px-4 text-[13px]`, and `lg` is `h-12 px-5 text-[14px]`.
- Dialog titles use `text-[15px] font-semibold`; descriptions use `text-[12px] leading-5`.
- Cards use `text-[15px] font-[720]` titles and `text-[12.5px] leading-5` descriptions.
- Tables use `text-[13px]` rows and `text-[11px]` uppercase column headers.
- Badges/chips use `text-[11px] font-semibold`.
- Inputs/selects use `h-9 text-[13px]` for compact operational forms.
- Logs/evidence use `font-mono text-[11.5px]` or `font-mono text-[12px]`.

## Status Classes

Use the same source-app status families:

- `bg-violet-50 text-violet-700 border-violet-200` for primary OPS stage emphasis.
- `bg-blue-50 text-blue-700 border-blue-200` for running/info states.
- `bg-indigo-50 text-indigo-700 border-indigo-200` for grouped/coordinator states.
- `bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-700 border-emerald-200` for successful, healthy, or completed states.
- `bg-amber-50 text-amber-800 border-amber-200` for delayed, investigating, or at-risk states.
- `bg-rose-50 text-rose-700 border-rose-200` for failed, open-risk, or destructive states.
- `bg-cyan-50 text-cyan-700 border-cyan-200` for auto-resolved or platform automation.

## Components

- Use shadcn/Radix primitives from `src/components/ui/` for generic UI controls: button, card, badge, dialog, sheet, popover, tooltip, tabs, table, input, textarea, select, switch, skeleton, and toast.
- Build OPS-specific components in `src/components/ops/` by composing those primitives.
- Raw HTML elements are acceptable only for semantic structure or inside local primitives/composites where the native element is the correct accessible base, such as navigation links, clickable metric cards, collapsible group headers, and row-name buttons.
- Do not introduce another component library.
- Keep OPS pages dense: 10-14px operational copy, compact controls, tables, grouped panels, and tight metadata.
- Cards are for repeated items, panels, dialogs, drawers, and framed tools. Do not nest cards inside cards.
- Use icon buttons for tool actions when a known icon exists; pair text with icons for clear commands.
- Put page-specific actions beside the page title or filter tabs, aligned to the right; keep global search and navigation in the app shell. Compact pages without a content heading may keep their actions in the shell.
- Use the external-link icon only for destinations outside NerveCenter. Same-app links, including links opened in a new tab, use an action-specific icon when one is needed or no navigation icon when the action stands alone.
- Keep focus rings visible with violet/brand focus treatment.
- Use `OperationalStatus` (`statusMeta`) for every backend state shown to a person; it owns the label vocabulary and tones (blue = open, amber = in progress/late, violet = needs human review, rose = failed, emerald = successful, slate = closed/setup). Never colour a status by hand.
- Use `PriorityBadge` for P1–P4 (`variant="label"` in tables). P1 = most critical.
- Use `StatTile` inside `StatTileGrid` for every number row. Tiles are flat white with a hairline border; colour appears only on a non-zero figure. No gradients, no coloured icon chips.
- Use `StatStrip` + `StatCell` when three or four numbers belong to one subject (a domain panel on Overview). Cells are joined by hairlines and read as one row; each links to the filtered view behind it. Use `StatTile` grids for standalone number rows on list pages.
- Charts live in `src/components/ops/charts/`. `ColumnChart` (stacked columns plus an optional line on the same axis, hairline grid, hover crosshair and tooltip, legend for two or more series) and `CompositionBar` (one bar split into named parts with counts). Only chart what a timestamp can prove; never a dual axis, never a pie. Colour lives on the marks; all text is ink. Columns grow once on mount; new values for the same buckets reshape the drawn marks in place (CSS transitions on `d`), and a new set of buckets redraws. Nothing loops.
- Knowledge (SPEC-knowledge-04 to 07). A base's **Map** view (`features/knowledge/KnowledgeMap.tsx`, layout in `knowledgeMapLayout.ts`) is a data visualisation, not decoration: shelves are sectors of one circle sized by page count, pages are points on rings inside their sector, typed links are curves bent towards the centre. Colour is state only (violet needs review, amber past its review date, grey kept, brand for the selection and its links); hovering or picking a page lights its neighbourhood and fades the rest; the side panel reads the page's fields and links. Points grow in once (`map-node`), links draw in once (`chart-line`). The list page's **Journeys** panel shows each eval score as a `ScoreRing` (emerald 80+, amber 60–79, rose under 60) that fills once (`score-ring`) beside its change and `Sparkline`; **This week** shows the weekly pass switch and the digest as `StatCell` tiles that open their list through `?digest=`. A correction Sonic saved shows in the turn as the violet "Saved for review" `TeachCard`.
- Use `TimeRangePicker` + `useTimeRange` for the reporting window. One window for the whole app (URL `range=` wins, then storage). Presets: Today · Last 12 h · Last 24 h · Last 7 days · Custom. Open/live items are never hidden by the window; only finished work is scoped.
- Use `OpsTable` (`OpsHead/OpsTh/OpsRow/OpsTd/TitleCell`) for lists. 40px rows, uppercase 10.5px headers, sortable columns via `useTableSort`. Card lists are not used for repeated operational items.
- Use `SectionHeader` + `ViewAllLink` above a table section. No narrative notes under headings.
- Use `describeSchedule` for cron or schedule strings so users read "At 02:00, only on Saturday", never `0 2 * * 6`.
- Any list or grid that can hold more than 10 items pages with `Pager` and `usePager` (SPEC-platform-11): 10 · 25 · 50 · 100 per page for tables and lists, 12 · 24 · 48 for card grids, page and size in the URL, back to page 1 when a filter or search changes. A list the server pages passes its `total` and asks for `offset` and `limit`. The bar is hidden while everything fits on the smallest size.
- List pages filter with search plus a few `Select`s, each labelled "Filter by …", state in the URL, and a "Clear N" button. Offer only filters a business user would use (priority, status, assignee, source); never internal ids such as the agent that handled a ticket.

## Agent components (SPEC-tasks-10)

- The task page is one thread: the outcome line under the title, then turns. A turn is the fold ("Worked for 2m · 11 steps · Databricks"), then (once finished) the reply's first sentence as a 17px semibold headline, the components Sonic drew in order, then the rest of the reply. No view tabs, no rail; files and session facts live in the Details sheet.
- Every component is `AgentCard` (a `SurfaceCard`) or, for notes and actions, unframed. A composed view (`show_view`) has a heading and no frame of its own, so its blocks are never cards inside a card; half-width blocks sit two to a row, one column in the panel (`compact`).
- Colour only on states, tones and numbers: `StateTag` (good, bad, warning, neutral, brand) for a state word, the status families for cells and dots, `seriesColor` for chart series. Charts use `components/ops/charts`; never a new chart kit. The richer visualisations (`cards/VizBlocks.tsx`: heatmap, gauges, funnel, gantt) follow the same rules: sequential shading is one hue in five steps, a state colour always comes with its word, every mark has a hover label and an `aria-label`, marks are thin with 4px rounded ends and a 2px surface ring where they meet.
- Components are data. A renderer reads its input through the parser in `cards/blocks.ts` (caps lengths, defaults states, drops bad rows); a kind without a renderer shows as `GenericBlockCard` (facts, lists, tables), never as nothing or an error. Nothing Sonic sends is set as HTML.
- Actions never write. `ask` sends a message as the reader through `TaskActionsContext`; it is disabled, with the reason as its title, while the task runs, waits, is stopped, or for viewers. `open` follows an app route, a task file or an `https://` link (`target="_blank" rel="noreferrer"`).
- Approvals in the turn use the warning tone. With an explanation, lead with Sonic's summary, label it as such, and keep the exact command behind "Show command".
- A tested fix (SPEC-tasks-13) draws what NerveCenter measured, never the model's input: the proposal card (tests passed of total, failed tests in red first, the table change, the checks, one "Now | After fix" pair per widget drawn with the board's own `WidgetBody`, the statement behind "Show statement"), the same evidence on the `apply_fix` approval, then the result card ("Expected | Actual" and a Verified / Check failed / Not verified tag). "After fix" and "Actual" sides carry the brand outline; colour stays on the tags.
- Asking about what is on screen (SPEC-tasks-12) uses `components/ops/AskSonic.tsx`: Sonic's mark as a 24px icon button (on hover for board widgets, always on Overview panels) and the picked-point bar ("COMM · units 5 · Ask Sonic about this · ×"). The panel shows the selection as one "About" chip above the box. No other "Ask AI" styling.
- Adding a component: one entry in `backend/src/tasks/ui-catalog.mjs` (tool, shape, validation, prompt line), a parser in `cards/blocks.ts`, a renderer in `cards/` wired in `BlockCard`, and a test in `catalog.test.tsx`.

## Copy

- Copy is data, not narrative. Page subtitles are one factual scope line. Tile support text is numbers or a period ("3 P1 · 5 P2", "In the last 24 hours").
- Empty states are a few words: "No open work", "No notifications", "All checks passing".
- No exclamation marks, no agent storytelling on operational screens, no "Loading…" text (use skeletons).
- Sentence case everywhere; uppercase only for 10.5px table headers.

## Motion

- 200–300ms on `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`. Allowed: `stagger-in` on tile/row mount (30ms apart, first 12 items), 1px hover lift with `shadow-card-hover`, segmented-control thumb slide, live-dot pulse on moving work, popover/sheet enter.
- Every figure arrives the same way on every page: `AnimatedNumber` / `AnimatedFigure` count it up from zero when it first appears and run it to a new value when it changes (650ms ease-out). Plain numbers, money and percentages only; dates and "in 3h" stay text.
- Grids and lists of tiles, cards and rows rise in one after another with `stagger-children` on their container (35ms apart, first twelve); table rows do the same through `OpsRow index`. A settings tab fades in like a page.
- On a board, a widget whose figures change under the same filters (a refresh, Sonic's build) shows a brand ring that fades over 1.6s (`widget-updated`); a filter the reader picked does not light anything. While new figures load, the old ones stay and a thin brand sweep runs along the filter bar.
- A section's page fades up 6px on arrival (`page-enter`, backwards fill only so no transform lingers). Moving within a section (a ticket opening over the list) does not replay it.
- New task is the one expressive screen: a soft brand glow, orbit rings with slow dots around Sonic's mark and a fading dot grid behind the heading (`NewTaskBackdrop`), rotating example placeholders, and a turning brand ring on the focused box (`composer-glow`). Everything else stays quiet.
- Messages typed while Sonic works dock on top of the message box as a numbered queue (`QueuedMessages` via `TaskComposer above`), one header for the stack, actions on hover. The Sonic panel uses the one-row composer (`layout="inline"`).
- Not allowed: bouncing, animated gradients outside New task, decorative pulses.
- Everything respects `prefers-reduced-motion` via the global block in `index.css`.

## Behavior Rules

- Metric cards can navigate and apply filters.
- Monitor grouping and table modes are both first-class.
- Detail drawers are read-first, with contextual ticket/workbench actions.
- Ticket creation must detect duplicates before creating new mock tickets.
- Toasts confirm refresh, duplicate detection, and ticket creation.
- Empty, loading, and error states must be present for every future route that depends on external data.
- One task engine (`/api/tasks/*`) runs every agent chat: the task page and the Sonic panel show the same thread. Neither fakes a reply; both show the agent run until the answer lands.
