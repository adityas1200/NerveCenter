# Ticket handling

- Move work to In Progress only after ownership and required inputs are clear.
- Record evidence and links rather than pasting long logs into a comment.
- Do not close a material incident or access request until the intended outcome has been verified.
- When another team owns the blocker, state the dependency, owner, and next checkpoint.
