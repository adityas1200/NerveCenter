# Incident updates

An incident update should reduce uncertainty without claiming more than the evidence supports.

Include:

- current status and customer or business impact;
- affected and unaffected scope when known;
- what changed since the previous update;
- mitigation or investigation currently underway;
- known risk and important unknowns;
- next update time or decision checkpoint.

Use exact times with timezone. Distinguish detected, contained, recovering, restored, and verified. Do not publish a root cause during an active incident unless it is established and reviewed; use "working hypothesis" when appropriate.

When the incident is resolved, state what was verified and what follow-up remains. Link the detailed RCA rather than placing speculative analysis in a broad message.

- [Google Cloud incident communication](https://docs.cloud.google.com/service-health/docs/incident-communication)
