PASS if the plan discovers first (charter, connected systems, the ticket export audited by grouping repeated problems) before asking anyone, drafts into a named structure with sources and draft status, and turns only the gaps into interview questions. The questions must be specific and evidence-anchored (for example naming the two "reran job" incidents and offering likely causes as options plus "don't remember"), put facts it already believes as confirm-or-correct, fit roughly 15 to 20 per hour grouped by theme, and say how transcripts will be matched to questions, checked for misheard names, and cited by file, timestamp and speaker.
FAIL if it plans a long list of broad open questions ("describe your orchestration"), asks the team to fill in forms, copies tickets or transcripts into knowledge, marks anything approved, or treats one person's statement as final without review.
