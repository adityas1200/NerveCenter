# Access-control principles

- Grant only the minimum authorization needed for the stated work.
- Resolve a durable subject and entitlement identity before action.
- Separate request, approval, execution, and verification where risk warrants it.
- Distinguish direct assignment from effective access inherited through groups, roles, policies, or nested membership.
- Treat privileged, break-glass, and production access differently from ordinary access.
- Prefer time-bounded access and an explicit review or expiry when the need is temporary.
- Check separation-of-duty conflicts and prerequisites.
- Record who approved, what changed, where, when, and what read-back proved.
- Revocation is not complete while equivalent residual access remains.

Reference: [NIST least privilege](https://csrc.nist.gov/glossary/term/least_privilege).
